AS-IS PROCESS

Step 1: Customer Browses Products
Customers visit the online store and browse automotive apparel items.
Products are displayed in a catalog/grid
Each product includes:
Name
Price
Image
Description
Pain point (real-world):
 Customers may abandon if UI is slow or unclear.

Step 2: Customer Selects Product
Customer clicks on a product to view details.
Product detail page opens
Customer reviews item information

Step 3: Add to Cart
Customer adds product to shopping cart.
Cart stores selected items
Quantity may be adjusted

Step 4: Checkout Initiation
Customer proceeds to checkout.
Shipping details entered
Contact details provided

Step 5: Payment Processing
Customer completes payment using external payment gateway.
Credit card / PayPal / other methods
System validates transaction

Step 6: Order Confirmation
Once payment is successful:
Order is created in backend system
Customer receives confirmation email
Order number is generated

Step 7: Fulfillment
Store processes the order:
Warehouse picks item
Item is packaged
Item is shipped

Step 8: Delivery & Completion
Customer receives product.
Order marked as complete
Tracking updates provided
